Design Guidelines

  • Dimensions: 1400 x 200 pixels (7:1 aspect ratio - ultra-wide banner)
  • Format: JPEG, PNG, or WebP
  • Max File Size: 5MB
  • Text Size: Minimum 40px for readability
  • Colors: High contrast for better visibility
  • Safe Zone: Keep content within 1200x180px center area
  • Mobile: Text should be readable when scaled to 350px width
  • Layout: Horizontal/landscape design works best

Quick Tips

  • Use bold, sans-serif fonts for better readability
  • Limit text to 1-2 lines for maximum impact
  • Include your logo or branding in the corner
  • Use gradients or solid colors for backgrounds
  • Avoid busy patterns that make text hard to read
  • Test your design on both light and dark screens

Software Recommendations

Free Tools:

  • Canva: Create banner at 1400x200px (free templates available)
  • GIMP: Professional image editor (open-source)
  • Figma: Design tool with precise dimensions
  • Photopea: Online Photoshop alternative

Quick Start with Canva:

  1. Go to Canva.com
  2. Click "Custom Size"
  3. Enter 1400 x 200 pixels
  4. Choose a template or start from scratch
  5. Design with horizontal/wide layout in mind
  6. Download as PNG or JPEG
💡 Pro Tip: After creating your banner, optimize it using TinyPNG or Squoosh to reduce file size without losing quality!
